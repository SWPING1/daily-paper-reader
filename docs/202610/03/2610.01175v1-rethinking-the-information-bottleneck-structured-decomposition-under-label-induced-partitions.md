## 摘要
标准信息瓶颈（IB）正则化通过单一标量 I(Z;X) 约束表示，隐含地将所有信息视为同质的。然而，单一的全局压缩控制会将与标签相关的结构与条件内的残差变异耦合在一起，而不是独立地调节二者的分配，从而使无关信息得以保留在学习到的表示中。例如，在医学影像应用中，残差变异通常来源于采集条件、背景因素或受试者特有的外观。这一问题在数据有限的情形下尤为突出，此时模型容易过拟合此类变异，从而阻碍泛化。尽管现有的正则化方法可以稳定训练、控制容量或塑造表示几何，但它们并未明确地将类无关变异与支撑任务的结构分离开来。为解决这一局限，我们从基于标签诱导划分的结构化视角重新审视 IB，其中条件级结构与条件内信息扮演着不同的角色。这引出了一个双瓶颈形式：标准 KL 项控制全局信息容量，而条件 KL 项则针对条件内信息。我们证明，条件 KL 可以精确分解为条件内信息项与先验失配项，从而解释其与设计目标的一致性。采用单纯形结构的条件先验时，该方法可提供可控的潜在几何，并可无缝集成到现有流程中。在分类与分割任务上的实验表明，该方法在低数据分类中增益最为明显，并在密集预测基准上取得了一致的改进。

## Abstract
Standard information bottleneck (IB) regularization constrains representations via a single scalar I(Z;X), implicitlytreating all information as homogeneous. However, a single global compression control couples label-relevant structurewith residual within-condition variation, rather than regulating their allocation independently, allowing nuisanceinformation to persist in learned representations. For example, in medical imaging applications, residual variation oftenstems from acquisition conditions, background factors, or subject-specific appearance. This issue becomes particularlypronounced in data-limited settings, where models tend to overfit such variation, hindering generalization. While existingregularization methods can stabilize training, control capacity, or shape representation geometry, they do not explicitlyseparate nuisance-like variation from task-supporting structure. To address this limitation, we revisit IB from a structuredperspective based on a label-induced partition, where condition-level structure and within-condition information playdistinct roles. This leads to a dual-bottleneck formulation: a standard KL term controls global information capacity, while aconditional KL term targets within-condition information. We show that the conditional KL admits an exact decompositioninto a within-condition information term and a prior-mismatch term, explaining its alignment with the design objective.With a simplex-structured conditional prior, the method provides controllable latent geometry and integrates seamlesslyinto existing pipelines. Experiments on classification and segmentation show the clearest gains in low-data classificationand consistent improvements across dense prediction benchmarks.
